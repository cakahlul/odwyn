# Workflow recovery

Restarts interrupt active tasks and workflows. Review saved messages and deliverables, then resume the interrupted step. Completed steps and their outputs remain saved. Retrying keeps the room goal and compatible owner updates.

Before repeating a submission, send, purchase or deletion, inspect the current browser or workspace state. Interrupted commands may have partially changed files. Host workspace files persist after tasks end; no isolation snapshots or automatic source rollback are maintained.

Room artifact revisions reset confirmations and reviews. Complete the current review gates before publishing or passing selected deliverables into the next stage. Failed automatic transitions remain visible for owner review.
